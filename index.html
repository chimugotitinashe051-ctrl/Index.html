What is Pokieboii Market?

Pokieboii Market is a direct ad and product promotion portal built for instant buyer-to-seller connection.

Sellers / Admins: Can instantly upload photos from device gallery or files, add price tags, contact numbers, and list items for sale.
Customers: Browse verified ads, compare prices, and connect directly via instant Call or WhatsApp buttons.
Real-time Updates: Uploaded items sync immediately across device sessions and browsers.
